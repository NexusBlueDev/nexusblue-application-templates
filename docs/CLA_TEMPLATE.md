## Individual Contributor License Agreement

**"You"** means the individual who submits a Contribution to NexusBlue.

You represent that:
- You are legally entitled to grant the above licenses.
- Each Contribution is Your original creation.
- Your Contribution does not violate any third party's intellectual property rights.
- If Your employer has rights to intellectual property that You create, You have received permission to make Contributions on behalf of that employer.

### 5. No Obligation

You acknowledge that NexusBlue is under no obligation to use or incorporate Your Contributions.

By submitting a Contribution, You agree to the terms of this Agreement.
